TV Remote Support

Help for connecting and using TV Remote: Wi-Fi Control.

Can’t find your TV?

Turn on a compatible Samsung Tizen TV, an LG NetCast 3.0/4.0 TV from 2012–2013, or a select older Philips TV with JointSPACE v1 enabled. Connect the iPhone to the same trusted, non-guest Wi-Fi and allow TV Remote to access the local network in iPhone Settings. Routers with client isolation may prevent discovery. Current LG webOS and Philips models are not supported.

Can’t pair?

Keep the TV awake and scan again. Samsung TVs may show an approval request. Older LG NetCast TVs show a six-digit code to enter in the app. For supported older Philips TVs, activate JointSPACE by entering 5646877223 with the original remote while watching TV, then scan again. Legacy LG NetCast and Philips JointSPACE v1 use unencrypted local HTTP, so use these connections only on a trusted private home network. If a Samsung TV asks you to trust its local certificate, verify the name and local address before accepting.

A button did not work?

TV Remote currently supports compatible Samsung Tizen TVs, older LG NetCast 3.0/4.0 models from 2012–2013, and select older Philips TVs exposing JointSPACE v1. Current LG webOS and Philips models are not included. On the legacy LG and Philips adapters, app shortcuts, iPhone text entry, and standby wake are not available. Playback and input behavior varies by model and app. The app does not report whether the television actually changed state.

Contact

Email TV Remote Support

When contacting support, include your TV model, iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what you tapped. Do not include passwords, pairing tokens, or private network information.
