TV Remote Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

What the app does

TV Remote discovers compatible televisions on the local Wi-Fi network and sends remote-control commands to a television selected by the user. The app does not create an account or send information to a developer-operated server.

Local network information

During discovery and pairing, the app may read a television’s local IP address, device name, model, and hardware address. Discovery results and the selected television details stay on this iPhone. Remote commands are sent over the local network to the selected television.

Connections and command security

TV Remote sends commands only to the television you select on your local Wi-Fi. Samsung TVs use an encrypted connection when supported. Some compatible Samsung models may require an older, unencrypted local remote connection. The legacy LG NetCast and Philips JointSPACE v1 protocols also use unencrypted local HTTP. Use these connections only on a trusted private Wi-Fi network.

Keyboard text

Text entered in the app is sent only after the user presses Send, only to the selected television, and only over an encrypted Samsung connection. Text entry is disabled on unencrypted connections. The app does not receive confirmation of what the television displayed or did with the text. Apple keyboard and dictation features follow the user’s iPhone settings and Apple’s privacy terms.

On-device storage and deletion

TV details and app preferences are stored on the iPhone. Pairing credentials and the trusted television certificate fingerprint are kept in the iOS Keychain and are not sent to the developer. To delete a saved TV and its pairing credentials, open Settings → Manage TVs → Forget TV.

Tracking, analytics, and sharing

TV Remote includes no advertising, analytics, tracking, account, or developer-server SDKs. The developer does not collect or sell personal information. The app does not share your local-network information with the developer.
